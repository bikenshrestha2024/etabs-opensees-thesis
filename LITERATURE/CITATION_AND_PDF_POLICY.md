## PDF status
`Free Access` publisher means a free reading/downloading route; it does not automatically authorize a public GitHub mirror. Verify license text specifically. An article published by a journal under a Creative Commons license may permit public redistribution *subject to attribution and license conditions*. Only confirmed licensed PDFs can enter public repo `LITERATURE/OPEN_ACCESS_PDFS/`; record original source, license and SHA256.

The five uploaded PDFs are preserved as **private user-provided research copies**. They are **not** publicly committed. Do not source PDFs from unofficial pirated sites. Provide links to publisher PDF interfaces and official institutional repositories.

## Field completeness
The master index records full author/title/journal/date only where verified from provided first-page PDF or publisher metadata. `pending verification` signals a real gap; do not silently turn years into calendar dates. Link publication DOI using `https://doi.org/<doi>`.

Bibliography is scientific *source provenance*, not approval to transfer FVD stiffness, Steel02 parameters, wall concrete confinement, or reinforcement to the ETABS model.
