## Coordinated research experiments — preserve existing baseline

1. **Steel material first.** Run independent monotonic/cyclic Steel02 and SteelMPF coupons with correct Menegotto-type transition normalization. Preserve existing nominal Fe500 card and document material/units/source identities. Frame and wall constitutive maps are different.
2. **Then RC elements.** Reproduce single-column/one-bay moment–curvature and lateral cyclic response using current sections. Study fiber/frame integration and discretization under fixed loading. Compare Mander and Kent–Park concrete envelopes with actual confinement, distinguishing numerical and parameter uncertainty.
3. **Then wall first-step investigation.** Keep source geometry and load/restraint/mass unchanged; inspect suspect wall tag 110280 and exact node connectivity, support choice 32/76/97 and gravity initial state. Run ZERO, X, Y, XY with exact accepted and failed timestamps. Wall E-SFI/FSAM is **not** qualified by a Steel02 frame coupon.
4. **Then earthquake records.** Preserve waveform SHA and source station, component, matched scale and duration; compute spectrum and Tm/Tp diagnostics, no hidden extra matching. Native XY/YX and timestep comparisons required.
5. **Then M0–M6 performance.** Compare accepted full duration and drift, corner torsion, roof response, device force/velocity/stroke/work, local wall/frame response and energy. Only afterward explore optional P-58 consequences if inventory and fragilities exist.
