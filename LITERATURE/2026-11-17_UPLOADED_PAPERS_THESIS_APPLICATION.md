## Work packages and thesis evidence contract

**W1—Nonlinear damping and first-step integrity (P01, P04).** Make a research-only comparison of selected OpenSees Rayleigh alphaM, betaK, betaKInit and betaKcomm choices with identical source mass/stiffness and element regions. Verify zero-input response, modal damping at two periods, hinge force/moment/damping-energy and full-record integration. Track element tag 110280 and the last accepted finite state before a failing step. Avoid declaring initial stiffness damping intrinsically correct or universally incorrect without source-verified analysis.

**W2—Asymmetric placement and device physics (P02, P03, P11).** Keep M0–M6 unchanged. Compare equal-count and equal-physical-capacity FVD layouts at re-entrant vs external perimeter directions, preserving same event, spectrum and source geometry. Report maximum X/Y drift, roof motion, corner displacements, torsional indices, story/floor acceleration, peak device force, relative velocity/stroke and device hysteresis/work. The dimension of FVD C in `F=C|v|^alpha sign(v)` depends on alpha: confirm mm/s-to-m/s conversion and native ETABS/OpenSees unit consistency before changing C. TVMD studies, if added, must be separate inertance/tuning-spring models.

**W3—Reinforcement/material uncertainty (P12–P15).** Derive core/cover concrete materials from exact hoop/cross-tie details and observed material grades. Verify the stress–strain, moment–curvature and cyclic loop against analytical predictions and independent specimens. Distinguish frame Concrete01/Steel02 paths from actual wall ConcreteCM/SteelMPF/FSAM path. Keep historical 1972 argument papers as alternative assumptions, not calibrated test cases.

**W4—Seismic data and code (P05, P09, P16).** Store event ID, station and component pair, raw/matched SHA, unit, dt, time length, PGA/PGV, spectrum and scale. Reject pair mismatch and double scaling, and separately review near-field pulses and component rotation. Use NBC 105:2025 as current Nepal code source, EN 1998-1:2004 for comparison only. Paired XY and YX runs must be genuinely complete.

**W5—Nepal context and eventual performance/loss use (P06–P08, P10, P17).** Separate motivation/field failure modes from component-level constitutive evidence; after full calibrated NLTHA, optional FEMA P-58 consequences need occupancy, nonstructural inventory, fragility and consequence functions rather than assumed damage costs.

**W6—Outstanding source equivalence.** Match exact ETABS six-DOF support/analysis-joint topology for 32 vs 76 vs 97 support scenarios, integrated wall base force/moment, diaphragm/shell load transfer, rigid end offsets and full source loads before accepting reaction comparisons. Passing total equilibrium is insufficient.
