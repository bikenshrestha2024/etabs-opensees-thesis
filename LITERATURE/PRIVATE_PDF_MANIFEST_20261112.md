**Original source insights relevant to engineering model:**
- Mander theoretical paper (1988) identifies confining reinforcement configuration, peak compressed concrete stress and strain; Mander experimental companion (1988) tests rectangular wall/column sections and confinement behavior. Neither alone qualifies a full project's ConcreteCM wall law.
- Chopra and McKenna (online 2015, journal volume 2016) warn about spurious Rayleigh damping forces in nonlinear response history analysis and recommend modal damping for the considered formulations. Their paper does not mean a model using modal damping will automatically converge.
- Chen et al. (2010) derive idealized damped-outrigger modal-position and damping-size relationships for a simplified beam-damper model, not direct 25-storey asymmetric RC modeling calibration.
