## Task 3: §5.2 mid-height rule in the storey seismic-weight ledger

Editor Claude Code, model UNKNOWN.

Behavioural change
- `components/model/assembly.py` story ledger (`NBC105_ESM_Story_Weights_Assembled_V118.csv`) previously assigned each active node's weight to the first floor at or above its elevation, so all weight between two floors went to the upper floor. It now uses `storey_index_for_elevation` (new, `components/modal/nbc105_clause5.py`): weight below the mid-height between adjacent floors goes to the lower floor, otherwise to the upper floor (NBC 105:2025 §5.2, printed p. 30). Nodes at or below the first floor stay with floor 1 (total preserved; base is not a lateral level); nodes at or above the roof go to the roof.
- Effect: story weights Wi shift between adjacent floors (typically weight moves from each floor to the one below). Total ledger weight and `story_ledger_mass_error_percent` are unchanged (assignment is a partition of the same nodes).
- Project exports must be re-assembled for the change to take effect. Previously saved CSVs and `Saved_Project_V156/` are not modified and still carry the old assignment.
- Other sites with a similar "first floor at or above" rule were left alone because they do not build Wi: `components/gravity/storey_loads.py` (applied-load breakdown by level, explicitly labelled as such) and `components/ui/history_inspection.py` (displacement interpolation between levels).

Existing tests whose pinned numbers were touched: none (no existing test pinned story-weight values; full suite passed unchanged). New tests: `T5::StoreyIndexTests`, `callsites::AssemblyUsesMidHeightRule`.
