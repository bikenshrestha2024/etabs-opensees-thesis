# 32-support gravity conversion review

**Status:** The best controlled probe reduced the maximum point error from 3,041.266 to 756.511 kN. The native ETABS reaction comparison remains open.

## Comparison and evidence

This is the ETABS 23.2.0 full-gravity case `1. DL + LL`, using the Assignment and Analysis Export ULS 3 storey workbooks and the verified clear-beam V118 OpenSees model. The reference total is 311,367.3136 kN. The OpenSees total is 311,367.313880 kN. OpenSees 3.8.0 ran every probe with the same mesh, 32 direct restraints, nodal gravity vectors and full load factor. The one-step diagnostic baseline reproduced the saved ten-step reactions within 0.01 kN at every support.

| In-memory variant | Delta at joints 263 and 264, each (kN) | Maximum absolute error at any support (kN) | Sum of absolute errors (kN) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Saved gross-wall formulation, reproduced | +3,041.266 | 3,041.266 | 37,297.027 |
| ETABS wall membrane factors | −179.490 | 825.043 | 10,107.659 |
| Wall membrane factors and frame shear areas | −173.336 | 820.119 | 10,068.779 |
| Wall membrane factors, frame shear areas and section modifiers, with frame geometric stiffness enabled | −92.354 | **756.511** | **9,820.706** |

Positive delta means OpenSees carries more vertical reaction than ETABS. In the best probe the worst point is ETABS joint 261. The reduction is approximately 75% in the maximum error and 97% at the two original critical joints. This still exceeds the project’s 5–10 kN comparison target. The investigation did not tune individual reactions or use ETABS forces as OpenSees loads.

## How the OpenSees mesh carries gravity

1. The converter subdivides source wall and slab polygons into conforming quadrilaterals, projects source and floor-grid points onto panel directions, and splits frames where the mesh meets them. A shared solver tag means incident elements contribute to the same six global nodal DOFs. Coincident coordinates alone do not connect different node tags. The snapshot has 3,400 wall and 7,000 floor `ShellMITC4` cells. The ETABS analysis export lists 375 wall and 350 floor analysis elements.
2. In the **32-node** diagnostic, `fix` is applied to 14 reaction-bearing shell-corner locations and 18 physical frame endpoints. The other base nodes remain in the OpenSees mesh and can still transmit loads through the attached elements. In the **76-node** run, 44 additional intermediate source points are fixed directly. In the **97-node** run, another 21 generated wall-base nodes are fixed directly. These three cases share the original gravity ledger.
3. In ETABS the 44 intermediate source points have six restraint flags, zero reported vertical reaction and no direct physical frame or shell-corner incidence. Their `@LC` records connect them along parent shell edges. CSI describes this as flexible edge interpolation that transfers forces to the corners. For example, the intermediate point 725 lies 1.2 m along the 6 m edge between 261 and 262. Its geometric weights are 0.8 and 0.2. Full ETABS rotation and reaction-recovery conventions require additional verification. [CSI automatic shell edge constraints](https://docs.csiamerica.com/help-files/etabs/Menus/Assign/Shell/Auto_Edge_Constraints.htm) · [CSI Analysis Reference](https://docs.csiamerica.com/manuals/etabs/Analysis%20Reference.pdf) · [OpenSees ShellMITC4](https://openseespydoc.readthedocs.io/en/latest/src/ShellMITC4.html)
4. A local OpenSees 3.8.0 patch applied 100 kN in a single DOF at a point 20% along an edge. A weighted `equationConstraint` with two retained nodes and a penalty handler transferred 80 kN and 20 kN to the two ground spring supports. The DOF equation residual was approximately 1e-7 in model displacement units. The intermediate loaded node also returned 100 kN when queried with `nodeReaction`. Summing it with the two actual supports would count the load twice. `equalDOF` ties to one retained node. `ASDEmbeddedNodeElement` offers penalty interpolation using a surrounding domain. Both the constraint graph and reaction extraction must be validated on a shell patch before a whole-building replacement. [Equation constraint](https://opensees.github.io/OpenSeesDocumentation/user/manual/model/mp_constraint/equationConstraint.html) · [EqualDOF](https://opensees.github.io/OpenSeesDocumentation/user/manual/model/mp_constraint/equalDOF.html) · [ASD embedded node](https://opensees.github.io/OpenSeesDocumentation/user/manual/model/elements/ASDEmbeddedNodeElement.html)

The earlier 76-support result assigned 104,463 kN to the 44 interior nodes. With the membrane correction this fell to 86,565 kN, while the critical-corner error grew from approximately −10,914 to −11,565 kN. In the 97-support run, the corrected model assigns 62,148 kN to those 44 nodes and 24,877 kN to the 21 added nodes. This is why further 32-support work holds the 32 direct restraint locations fixed and investigates connectivity and stiffness independently.

## Conversion differences and what was tested

- **Wall stiffness:** the saved runtime takes the gross elastic wall branch. Wall 600 and several other assigned sections specify f11/f22/f12 = 0.50, m11/m22/m12 = 0.10 and v13/v23 = 1.00. The membrane-only test applies the source property membrane modifiers with the original wall plate stiffness. This explains the largest observed shift, but a source-equivalent section must represent all three terms independently. The layered probe used symmetric three-layer quadrature and independent transverse shear from PlateFromPlaneStress, while keeping physical gravity and mass unchanged. Its similar residual suggests that transverse shear coupling alone does not explain the remaining error. [CSI shell stiffness modifiers](https://docs.csiamerica.com/help-files/etabs/Menus/Assign/Shell/Shell_Stiffness_Modifiers.htm) · [OpenSees ElasticMembranePlateSection C++](https://github.com/OpenSees/OpenSees/blob/master/SRC/material/section/ElasticMembranePlateSection.cpp) · [OpenSees LayeredShell C++](https://github.com/OpenSees/OpenSees/blob/master/SRC/material/section/LayeredShellFiberSection.cpp)
- **Frame stiffness:** the baseline `elasticBeamColumn` uses centerline frame segments and omits ETABS shear areas and some section modifiers. The probe replaces 3,750 unreleased beam/column segments from 1,200 objects with `ElasticTimoshenkoBeam`, using As2/As3 converted from cm² to m² and the workbook A/J/I22/I33 modifiers. Braces retain their existing idealization. The `-geomNonlinear` repeat is important because the current OpenSees source does not infer the element's geometric stiffness flag from the transform type. The probe retains centerline application of the original nodal loads. Separate earlier offset tests were approximate and did not explain the original 3,041 kN difference. ETABS applies rigid end lengths to bending and shear while retaining full axial and torsional flexibility and reports forces at the offset faces. [CSI frame end offsets](https://docs.csiamerica.com/help-files/etabs/Menus/Assign/Frame/End_Length_Offset.htm) · [OpenSees Timoshenko implementation](https://github.com/OpenSees/OpenSees/blob/master/SRC/element/elasticBeamColumn/ElasticTimoshenkoBeam3d.cpp)
- **Local load transfer:** the separately checked Dead and Live totals match ETABS. That establishes global magnitudes only. The native edge constraints, floor mesh, frame insertion behavior, object modifiers, end-zone load application and story-by-story transfer still need a native analytical mesh and common force-output cuts to isolate the remaining point error. Prior geometric redistribution of saved 76-point reactions increased the critical error to +4,253 kN. ASDShellQ4 changed the predicted response but gave a higher error on the tested mesh. [ASDShellQ4 formulation](https://opensees.github.io/OpenSeesDocumentation/user/manual/model/elements/ASDShellQ4.html)

## Literature and repository scope

The GitHub repository search used the terms ETABS and OpenSees and was expanded with official manuals, current upstream source and recent literature. Major results include the converter for the enclosed example in [Goings et al., E2O-SEAOC2020](https://github.com/OpenSeesPro/E2O-SEAOC2020), the [CEO converter and methodology](https://peer.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/carlos_garcia_-_poster_-_peer_2019_-_final.pdf), [DINOETO for ETABS 9.6](https://github.com/dinochen1983/DINOETO), the [SHM geometry and time-history workflow](https://github.com/mohammadshamsice/Civil-Engineering-Department-SHM), and a recent [IFC steel-frame comparison workflow](https://github.com/Son012375/opensees-kds-bim-pipeline). The [project's V134 predecessor](https://github.com/bikenshrestha2024/etabs-to-opensees-outrigger) supplies lineage context.

The [PEER OpenSeesPy verification report](https://peer.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/publications/2019_06_zhu.pdf) provides elastic wall and patch benchmarks. The 2026 [component](https://github.com/kkolozvari/Data_Comparative_Assessment_of_NL_Analysis_Software_for_-PBD_of_RC_Buildings_Part_1) and [system](https://github.com/kkolozvari/Data_Comparative_Assessment_of_NL_Analysis_Software_for_PBD_of_RC_Buildings) comparison datasets provide independent benchmarking examples. [OpenSees 3.8.0 release notes](https://github.com/OpenSees/OpenSees/releases) identify the equation constraint addition verified in the local patch. Reviewed projects and papers do not certify this building’s gravity load path or the ETABS 23.2 edge-constraint translation.

## Acceptance decision

The tested stiffness changes are diagnostic evidence. The committed document records them without promoting an approximate model into the production defaults. The next discriminating comparison is a native analytical-mesh variant with explicit shell edge relations and source-matched section properties, while holding the direct foundation supports and load case fixed. Use 32 support reactions, 11 base wall-panel resultants and source beam support-face station forces as simultaneous checks. Publish source hashes with each result and retain the residuals even when global equilibrium passes.
