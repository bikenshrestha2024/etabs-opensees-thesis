## How to find gravity results

Use the **same selected output root** for the 32-, 76- and 97-support conditions.
After a completed run, open `Gravity_Output_Index.txt` in that root. Its companion
`Gravity_Case_Reaction_Index.json` records the exact per-condition result paths.
PDF, comparison and evidence actions use the selected condition, not the newest
file or an assumed parent-directory result.

Separate result subdirectories are retained to preserve condition identity.
Existing unindexed results are not automatically treated as current. Rerun the
desired condition after updating. Modified model/export fingerprints invalidate
indexed results; switching conditions cannot reuse another condition's reactions.
The 32-support condition remains a diagnostic, not an inferred foundation model.
